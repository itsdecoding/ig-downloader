Frequently asked questions

Which video links are supported?
Any public Instagram Reel/Video/Carousel, TikTok video (including vm.tiktok.com and vt.tiktok.com), or YouTube Shorts / YouTube video URL.
Why does YouTube Shorts sometimes take a second longer to download?
YouTube often separates 720p/1080p video and audio streams. When an HD split stream is available, our server muxes them into a single playable MP4 with FFmpeg so you don't get stuck at 360p.
Can I transcribe the video after downloading?
Yes — click the Transcribe button on the result card to send the same link straight into our Video to Text tool for timestamped Whisper transcripts.
What does Save to Workspace do?
It saves the clip title, source link, thumbnail, and caption into your Droplabs Workspace in one click with zero signup friction, so you can build a swipe file or content research board.
