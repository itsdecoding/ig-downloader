Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the timestamps?
Timestamps come directly from Whisper segment boundaries so every sentence or phrase lines up with the moment it is spoken in the video.
What is the maximum clip length or upload size?
Uploaded clips can be up to 50 MB, and linked videos up to 10 minutes. Everything is deleted from temporary server storage immediately after transcription finishes.
Why use FFmpeg before Whisper?
Stripping the video frames down to a 16 kHz mono MP3 shrinks a 40 MB video to under 1 MB of clean speech audio, which makes transcription much faster.
