Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading Instagram videos.

Is InstaFetch completely free to use?

Yes! InstaFetch is 100% free with no registration, paid tiers, or limits on the number of videos you can download.

Do I need to sign in with my Instagram account?

No. We never ask for your Instagram password, username, or login credentials. The entire process is anonymous and requires zero personal information.

Where do the downloaded videos get saved?

On iPhone / iPad, videos save to your Safari Downloads folder or the Files app. On Android and PC/Mac, files save directly into your system's Downloads folder.

Can I download multi-slide carousel videos?

Yes! If an Instagram post contains multiple video slides (album), InstaFetch detects each clip and lets you preview and download each one individually.

Does InstaFetch store copies of the downloaded videos?

No. InstaFetch does not host or store any media on its servers. All files are streamed directly from Instagram's content delivery networks to your device.
